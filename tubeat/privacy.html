Privacy Policy

Applies to tuBEAT (com.kurtsoft.tubeat) on Android · Effective 30 August 2026

tuBEAT is a player for media that is already on your device. It has no account, no sign-up and no advertising, and it sells nothing. This page describes exactly what the app reads, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

The short version. Your music and video files are read from your device and are never uploaded. Two things do leave the phone: the title and artist of a playing track, sent to look up lyrics and artwork, and crash reports and anonymous usage events, sent so the app can be fixed and improved. There is nothing else.

1. Who we are

tuBEAT is published by Kurtsoft Yazılım ve Teknoloji A.Ş., Antalya, Türkiye. For anything in this policy, write to destek@kurtsoft.com.tr.

2. What the app reads from your device

To do its job, tuBEAT needs to see the media you already have:

  • Audio and video files — read through Android's media permissions so the library can list them, read their tags and artwork, and play them. These files stay on your device; they are never copied to us or to anyone else.
  • Playlists you create — written as ordinary .m3u8 files in your own storage, so they remain yours if you uninstall the app.
  • Your settings and listening history — language, theme, playback options and play counts, all stored in the app's own storage on the phone.

Denying the media permission does not lock you out of the app; it simply means there is nothing for the library to show.

3. What leaves your phone

3.1 Lyrics and artwork lookup

When a track plays, the app can look up synced lyrics and cover art for it. To do that it sends the track title, artist name and track length to third-party lyrics and music-metadata providers over the internet. It does not send the audio file, the file path, or anything that identifies you or your device. If a lookup fails, nothing is stored and the app simply shows no lyrics.

3.2 Crash reports and usage analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous in-app events (for example “playback started”, “settings opened”). These carry technical information such as device model, Android version, app version, coarse region and a random, resettable installation identifier. They do not carry your name, your email, your file names or your library contents.

3.3 Remote configuration

Firebase Remote Config is used to change a few settings without shipping an update — for example turning a feature off if it misbehaves. Fetching that configuration sends the same installation identifier and app version described above.

3.4 Chromecast

If you cast, the app looks for Cast devices on your local network and streams the selected file to the device you pick. That traffic stays on your own network and does not pass through us.

5. How long anything is kept

  • On your phone: settings, playlists and listening history stay until you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes the app's own storage; playlists written to your shared storage remain, because they are your files.
  • Crash reports: retained by Google's Crashlytics for a limited period and then deleted automatically.
  • Analytics events: retained according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Lyrics lookups: not stored by us at all. The request is made, the answer is displayed, and that is the end of it.

6. Your choices

  • Stop the lookups: turn off internet access for the app, or use it in airplane mode. Playback of your own files is unaffected.
  • Reset the identifier: the installation identifier is regenerated if you clear the app's data or reinstall it.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app. To ask us to delete anything held on our side, write to destek@kurtsoft.com.tr and we will action it.

7. Children

tuBEAT is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, write to us and it will be removed.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your phone, the date at the top of this page changes with it and the updated version is published here before the new app version is released.
